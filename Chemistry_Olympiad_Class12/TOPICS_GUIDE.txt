                    🔥 PRIORITY 1: MUST-HAVE TOPICS
                (Generate these first - 70 questions)
================================================================================

ORGANIC CHEMISTRY:
------------------
📌 Aldehydes & Ketones (15 questions)
   Why important:
   - Nucleophilic addition mechanisms
   - Name reactions (Aldol, Cannizzaro, etc.)
   - High weightage in Olympiads
   - Connects to many other topics

   Key concepts to expect:
   • Nucleophilic addition mechanisms
   • Aldol condensation and reactions
   • Cannizzaro reaction
   • Oxidation and reduction
   • Distinguishing tests

📌 Reaction Mechanisms (10 questions)
   Why important:
   - Core understanding for all organic chemistry
   - SN1, SN2, E1, E2 mechanisms
   - Carbocation rearrangements

   Key concepts to expect:
   • SN1 vs SN2 comparison
   • E1 vs E2 elimination
   • Carbocation stability and rearrangements
   • Reaction kinetics and stereochemistry

PHYSICAL CHEMISTRY:
-------------------
📌 Chemical Kinetics (15 questions)
   Why important:
   - Mathematical problem-solving
   - Mechanism determination
   - Very common in IChO

   Key concepts to expect:
   • Rate law determination
   • Order of reactions
   • Arrhenius equation applications
   • Complex reaction mechanisms
   • Half-life calculations

📌 Electrochemistry (15 questions)
   Why important:
   - Nernst equation applications
   - Cell potential calculations
   - Practical applications

   Key concepts to expect:
   • Standard electrode potentials
   • Nernst equation problems
   • Electrolysis calculations
   • Battery chemistry
   • Conductance

INORGANIC CHEMISTRY:
--------------------
📌 Coordination Compounds (15 questions)
   Why important:
   - Very high weightage in Olympiads
   - Complex concepts (CFT, isomerism)
   - Requires deep understanding

   Key concepts to expect:
   • IUPAC nomenclature
   • Geometrical and optical isomerism
   • Crystal Field Theory
   • Magnetic properties
   • VBT vs CFT

================================================================================
                  💡 TIPS FOR EACH BRANCH
================================================================================

ORGANIC CHEMISTRY:
------------------
✓ Focus on mechanisms and electron movement
✓ Include synthesis problems (multi-step)
✓ Test understanding of reagents and conditions
✓ Cover stereochemistry aspects
✓ Include named reactions

Best question types:
• "Propose a mechanism for..."
• "Complete the synthesis using..."
• "Predict the major product..."
• "Arrange in order of reactivity..."

PHYSICAL CHEMISTRY:
-------------------
✓ Include numerical calculations
✓ Test understanding of graphs and plots
✓ Focus on derivations and applications
✓ Cover units and dimensional analysis
✓ Include data interpretation

Best question types:
• "Calculate the equilibrium constant..."
• "Given the following data, determine..."
• "The rate of reaction doubles when..."
• "Compare the following graphs..."

INORGANIC CHEMISTRY:
--------------------
✓ Test structure-property relationships
✓ Include color and magnetism
✓ Cover trends across periods/groups
✓ Focus on exceptions to trends
✓ Include crystal structures

Best question types:
• "Explain the magnetic behavior of..."
• "Which of the following is most stable..."
• "Draw the structure of..."
• "Arrange in order of: [property]..."


================================================================================
                    🔍 QUALITY MARKERS FOR GOOD QUESTIONS
================================================================================

A good Olympiad question should have:

✅ Clear problem statement
✅ Requires conceptual understanding (not just memory)
✅ Has one correct answer
✅ Plausible distractors (wrong options based on common mistakes)
✅ Detailed solution showing all steps
✅ Explains WHY the answer is correct
✅ Points out common mistakes
✅ Includes Olympiad-level insight

Avoid questions that:
❌ Can be answered by simple recall
❌ Are directly from textbook
❌ Have ambiguous wording
❌ Test obscure facts
❌ Have obvious answers
